Things to try

Questions to explore
  • Rotate the letter F by 45° counterclockwise. Does its area change? Compare with det A.
  • Reflect about the axis y = 0. Can you still read the F? What is the sign of det A?
  • Double the size of the figure. Why does the area grow by 4 and not by 2?
  • Project onto the line y = x. What is det A, and could any matrix undo this?
  • Place your picture far from the origin and rotate it. Why does it swing around the origin instead of turning in place?
  • Look at the columns of A and at the arrows Ae₁, Ae₂. They are the key to the mystery matrix challenge.
