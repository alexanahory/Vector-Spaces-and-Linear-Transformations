Things to try

Questions to explore
  • Start from x² + y² and move the slider b. For which values of b is the form still positive definite? Compare b² with ac.
  • Choose the example 5x² − 4xy + 8y² from the lecture notes. Check that the eigenvalues are 4 and 9 and that the axes are turned by about 26.6°.
  • Move the level k from 5 to −5 for a positive definite form. When does the level curve disappear, and how do you see it on the graph?
  • For an indefinite form, what is the level curve Q = 0? What happens to the hyperbola Q = k when k changes sign?
  • Compare x² and (x + y)². Both are positive semidefinite. On which line is Q = 0 in each case?
  • Multiply all three coefficients by −1. What happens to the graph, to the eigenvalues and to the classification?
  • Turn the graph to view it from above. Why does it look like the level-curve picture?
