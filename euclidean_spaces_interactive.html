Inner products on the plane

Every inner product on ℝ² has the form ⟨u, v⟩ = uᵀG v, where the Gram matrix G = [⟨ei, ej⟩] is symmetric and positive definite. Change G and the grid redraws itself so that every cell is a unit square for the new inner product. Proposition 2.3.5

Orthogonal decomposition in the plane

The projection of v on the line L = span{u} is the unique p = λu with ⟨u, v − p⟩ = 0. What is left over, v − p, lies on the orthogonal line L⊥. Both pieces, and the very meaning of “orthogonal”, depend on G. Definition 2.4.2

Orthogonal projection onto a plane

For a plane W through the origin, u = projW v is the unique vector of W with v − u ∈ W⊥. It is the point of W closest to v, so the distance from v to W is ‖v − u‖. Theorem 2.4.7
